# Decision traceability in TLA

TLA distinguishes model interpretability from organizational decision traceability.

A public organization may not be able to explain every internal mechanism of a complex AI model. It should still be able to reconstruct why it relied on an output and how the final decision was reached.

For consequential decisions, preserve where applicable:

1. the problem and decision owner;
2. evidence and sources;
3. assumptions and uncertainties;
4. relevant compiled knowledge;
5. comparable past experiences;
6. AI tools and significant configurations;
7. human interventions and validations;
8. experiments or observations;
9. final rationale;
10. conditions for review or expiration.

Compiled knowledge must not become its own evidence. Important claims should remain linked to external sources or identifiable real-world experience.
