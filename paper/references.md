## Reference policy for the first release

- Use primary publisher or official sources for bibliographic metadata.
- Do not cite a source unless the manuscript actually relies on it.
- Distinguish normative governance sources from empirical evidence.
- Do not use the Martinique illustrative case as if it were empirical validation.
