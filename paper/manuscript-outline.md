# TLA first research paper — manuscript architecture

## Working title

**TLA: A Territorial Learning Architecture for AI-Augmented Problem Solving — From Model-Centric AI to Cumulative Territorial Learning**

## Central research question

How can artificial intelligence be organized, together with human expertise and institutional experimentation, so that a territory does not merely obtain AI-generated answers but progressively acquires reusable problem-solving capabilities?

## Claimed contribution

The paper does **not** claim a new machine-learning algorithm.

Its contribution is an AI-system architecture and design framework with five linked ideas:

1. shift the unit of learning from the individual model to the human–AI territorial problem-solving system;
2. make decision-critical uncertainty an explicit object of representation and reduction;
3. bind generative and analytical AI to bounded evidence-generating micro-experiments;
4. preserve operational decision provenance without pretending that every foundation model is mechanistically interpretable;
5. accumulate validated learning as reusable territorial capabilities.

## Non-claims

The first paper must not claim:

- that TLA has already been deployed territorially;
- that it has demonstrated superiority over conventional governance;
- that LTAA or MIN have already produced measured field results;
- that model-internal reasoning is fully explainable;
- that Martinique is uniquely suited to the architecture.

## Intended status

Research preprint / technical paper accompanying an open-source research architecture.
