# Code of Conduct

TLA is an open research project. Contributions should be critical, evidence-oriented and respectful.

Participants are expected to:

- focus criticism on claims, evidence, architecture and implementation choices rather than individuals;
- distinguish clearly between established evidence, interpretation and speculation;
- disclose relevant limitations and conflicts of interest when they materially affect a contribution;
- avoid harassment, discrimination, personal attacks or bad-faith disruption;
- respect confidentiality, data protection and legal constraints when discussing territorial cases.

Maintainers may remove contributions or restrict participation when conduct undermines a safe and productive research environment.

Questions about research claims are welcome, including strong disagreement, provided the discussion remains substantive.
