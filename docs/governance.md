# Governance and drift control

TLA is intended for territorial and public-sector contexts. Legitimacy is therefore not treated as an optimization variable.

## Human authority

Human involvement should increase with:

- risk;
- irreversibility;
- normative content;
- contestability;
- legal or institutional significance.

Humans are not required because AI is always incapable. They are required where **authority, judgment, accountability or specialist expertise** belongs to people or institutions.

## Anti-drift rules

1. No important compiled knowledge without a recoverable link to external evidence.
2. Contradictions are preserved and signaled.
3. Syntheses are dated and versioned.
4. Knowledge states are traceable: proposed, corroborated, contested, validated, obsolete, invalidated.
5. High-impact decisions should preserve a reconstructable decision record.
6. AI-generated summaries must not become their own evidence.

## Micro-experimentation

A micro-experiment is an information-generating intervention, not a license to experiment freely on citizens.

Where rights, safety, access to services, personal data or other protected interests are affected, legal and democratic constraints take priority over informational value.
