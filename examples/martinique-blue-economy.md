# Illustrative design case — Blue economy in Martinique

> **Status:** illustrative design case, not empirical validation.

Question: **How can Martinique increase the local value generated by maritime activities without committing too early to heavy investment?**

A generative AI system can first propose an idea space: fishing, aquaculture, nautical tourism, biotechnology, energy, logistics or training.

TLA organizes what happens next.

## AER

AER asks what evidence is still missing and chooses a proportionate route:

- look for comparable prior experience;
- consult compiled knowledge about actors, infrastructure and regulation;
- return to original evidence through RAG for recent or contested facts;
- involve a human expert only when specialist competence or institutional authority is required;
- design a bounded micro-experiment only when the critical information does not yet exist.

## CAER

If comparable experience is found elsewhere, CAER compares source and Martinique contexts and chooses:

- **Reuse**
- **Adapt**
- **Re-experiment**

The goal is not to assume that a successful island case transfers automatically, but to identify which contextual difference may block reuse.

## Learning outcome

After action, the observed outcome is captured in Experiential Memory, including context, limitations, transfer conditions and residual uncertainty.

The objective is therefore not only to produce a recommendation on the blue economy, but to increase the territory's capacity to address subsequent decisions.
